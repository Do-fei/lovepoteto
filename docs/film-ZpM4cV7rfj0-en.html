2016 · EmberCamp London

I Can Write My App with No Handlebars

▶

Viewing notes

Where should filtering and sorting live when several templates need the same behavior? Tan compares different ways of expressing a list and then builds small helpers, including repeat and toggle. Look at each call from the perspective of the next person reading the template. The name identifies the operation, while its arguments reveal the data involved. The implementation can remain separately testable without obscuring what the screen does.
