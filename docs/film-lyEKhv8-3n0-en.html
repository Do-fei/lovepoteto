Viewing notes

Tan delivers the State of React segment in a shared keynote. A download chart gives a sense of the software’s reach, then a survey looks at people who are still learning to code. Watch the transition that follows: numbers give way to contributors’ faces and pictures of community events. Each image answers a different question about adoption. Distribution is measurable, while the work of teaching people and maintaining libraries is easier to see in the community itself.
