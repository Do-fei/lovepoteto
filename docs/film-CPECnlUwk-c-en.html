Swipe Left, Uncaught TypeError: Learning to Love Type Systems

Viewing notes

In the San Francisco recording, the small makeArray example illustrates a useful property of generics. A caller can keep information about the element it passed in even when the function works for many different types. Follow that example into the discussion of signatures that suggest implementations and then the closing section on GraphQL. Across the three stops, ask how much knowledge can survive the move from one layer of a program to another.
