Shruti Kapoor hosts a panel with Tan and members of the React team. Two audience questions work especially well together: what can the compiler infer from source code, and what should a team do with existing useMemo and useCallback calls? The first asks when an optimization is justified. The second puts that question into a codebase already in production. Other parts of the panel cover Activity, performance tooling and the React Foundation.
