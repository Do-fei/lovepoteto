What managing engineers taught her about agents

How I Use Cursor

Where to begin

A capable new engineer still needs to learn how a team investigates problems and decides a change is ready. Tan adds a complication for agents: a fresh session may have no recollection of yesterday’s dead ends.

A closer look

One example involves a scroll position that drifts periodically. Reproduction, competing explanations and a check after the fix give the investigation a shape that the next session can reuse. Parallel investigations can converge on that same difficult question.

Why Benny opens a ticket first

Benny follows up with the reporter, checks history and earlier discussion, and records what it learned in a ticket. A subsequent step attempts reproduction and repair. The split leaves room for an important discovery: an unexpected interface may be behaving exactly as it was designed to.

Editor’s notebook

A reusable team note should identify an observable signal. Recording the scroll position while the interface is idle gives a new session somewhere specific to start.
