Engineer to Manager and Back Again

▶

Viewing notes

When her director at Netflix offered her a management role, Tan asked for a week to find out what she would be taking on. Returning to engineering brought a different worry. Would her programming skills still be there? Listen for the distinction she draws between choosing the work you want to do each day and chasing a title that signals advancement. Her experiences giving feedback and speaking in public complicate the idea that growth runs along a single track.
