From Front End to Full Stack with Elixir and Phoenix

Viewing notes

Does moving an API to Phoenix require rewriting every route before shipping any of it? Tan builds up a view of a request as a pipeline, then presents a Plug named Terraform that forwards unhandled routes to the legacy API. That makes it possible to take responsibility for one endpoint at a time and check the behavior as the team learns. Terraform here is the name of the Plug in her example.
