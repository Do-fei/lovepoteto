Company build, day 1: Could a team of bots run a pop-up?

▶

Viewing notes

Lauren, Matt Palmer and Roshan Sadanani give themselves three days to start a company. Their first idea is a restaurant pop-up. Follow the work from the studio table to the first landing page. Bots can research venues, collect names and build a prototype while the hosts are still talking. A venue owner, a permit and a roomful of paying guests each have their own timetable. Those dependencies become more important to the experiment than the speed at which the team can ship a page.
