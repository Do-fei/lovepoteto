2018 · RubyConf AU

Building the World’s Largest Studio at Netflix

▶

Viewing notes

As Netflix began producing original content, documents and spreadsheets carried much of the coordination work. Tan describes the first Rails and Ember application that grew out of that process. Her account connects the changing needs of studio staff to readability and the team’s choice of Ruby. The later material on production tooling supplies the other half of the decision: a language can suit the people writing the application and still need deployment and monitoring support inside a larger engineering organization.
