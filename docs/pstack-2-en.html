Let the agent explain the problem first

The Complete Guide to pstack Pt. 2

Where to begin

A Slack thread tends to mix observations, theories and proposed fixes. Tan asks the agent to restate the issue before changing anything. She can hear where it went astray and leave room for an interpretation that challenges her own.

A closer look

While designing the Dune desktop framework, she started with a tutorial for someone building an app. A step that is hard to explain can reveal an awkward interface. Working backwards from the tutorial gives both parties a concrete scenario to test.

How a design earns its place

For a dropdown, Tan describes putting alternatives behind a switcher so interaction, layout and timing can be compared. For a service boundary, the useful artifacts are a caller’s code, types and signatures. Each comparison gives the person choosing a design something they can inspect at the point of use.

Editor’s notebook

Hide the proposed fix in a bug report and keep the observations. Ask two people to restate it independently. Their differences can expose an ambiguity before it becomes a patch.
