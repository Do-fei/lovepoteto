Ambitious UX for Ambitious Apps

▶

Viewing notes

How does a person know that a click did something while the application is still loading? Tan explores that question through a loading service and a queue of temporary messages. A service can own the state, a component can present it, and a timer can retire a message when its job is done. These small examples connect reusable Ember primitives with a recognizable experience of waiting for an interface to respond.
