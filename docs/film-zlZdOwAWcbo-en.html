From Front End to Full Stack with Elixir & Phoenix

Viewing notes

In this San Francisco recording, tuples and function specifications set up a small but consequential interface. A Plug takes a connection and options, then returns a connection. Once that contract is visible, incremental adoption is easier to picture. A Phoenix application can own selected paths while an existing service continues handling the rest. Each change of ownership gives the team a bounded piece of behavior to check.
