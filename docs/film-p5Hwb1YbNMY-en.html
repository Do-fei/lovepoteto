Viewing notes

Tan recalls pitching a technical idea and failing to persuade her manager. She rebuilds the proposal around the problem her colleagues actually face, the options available to them and the effect a change would have on their work. Later diagrams shrink the set of values a type can represent. That gives a skeptical listener something concrete to evaluate: a more precise type can rule out a particular mistake before it reaches a user.
