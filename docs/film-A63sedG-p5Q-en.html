Viewing notes

The most revealing part of this conversation with Roshan Sadanani is the handoff on screen. A coordinating bot asks another bot about previous work. Later, Tan dispatches bug reports to agents assigned to particular features. Her kitchen analogy then has a precise meaning: the stations can work independently, while someone still owns the decision that the plate is good enough to serve.
